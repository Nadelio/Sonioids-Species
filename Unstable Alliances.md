While rival [Factions](Factions.md)' members tend to avoid and distrust each other, it can happen that they end up forming an alliances if they consider it will benefits them.

These alliances are most of the time unstable as both parties won't trust the other. Alliances happens notably because of the difference in the way they were manufactured, some being more fit for one environment than another.

Because of that you can rarely see a mixed pair of researchers and explorers from different companies.
