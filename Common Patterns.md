# Common Patterns

Given that all Sonioids are from a couple different factions (companies), they usually have some form of patterning to help differentiate them. 

On top of this, they also contain a strip of textured metal along the back of their head that helps identify them to other Sonioids if verbal communication is not available. If verbal communication is available, they will send out a audio pattern that acts as an identifier for their faction (company).

Here are some examples of cool patterns that could be seen on a Sonioid (this is not an exhaustive list, if you don't like these patterns, feel free to come up with your own, noone is stopping you)

> [!NOTE]
> Generally, faction patterns are usually either sci-fi (see COD Cold War Science camos) or environment based (see TF2 warpaints)\
> Faction patterns can vary based on the material the sonioid is made of, the environment it is made for, the factions branding (colors, logos, common shapes used in logos or design elements), and also the people designing these sonioids (as in the engineers and scientists, not their factions)\
> Early sonioids don't have any patterns, and are instead a solid white or gray, depending on the era (the Mariana Trench sonioids are solid gray, while the early space-faring sonioids are solid white, and also have country of origin branding, like flags or seals).
