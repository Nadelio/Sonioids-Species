# Striker role

Striker is the umbrella term[^1] to describe combat Sonioids. They are generally fast and stealthy with the software and hardware to damage what they deem a threat

Built at first with refurbished [explorer](sonioids/Explorer%20role.md) chassies to perform industrial espionage, they later became an internal product line specialised in eavesdropping, tactical operations, and disruptance

Even tho [factions](factions/Factions.md) keep their existance top secret, the idea of a striker role natually emerged among many (if not all) factions. Such comparitmentalisation influenced striker sonioids development to have distinct and almost unique characteristics to the other faction's strikers. Given how expensive such a secret product line is factions have one striker chassie and only one, maybe two loadouts (loadout is not the term I would like to use because it seems something you can take on and off. What I mean is something that replaces a normal work arm with a heavy-duty clawed arm)

---

[^1]: Umbrella term because every Faction has a different nomenclature
