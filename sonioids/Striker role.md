# Striker role

Striker is the umbrella term[^1] to describe combat Sonioids. They are generally fast and stealthy with the software and hardware needed to damage what they deem a threat

Built at first with refurbished [explorer](sonioids/Explorer%20role.md) chassies to perform industrial espionage, they later became an internal product line specialised in eavesdropping, tactical missions, and strategical disruptance of opponents' supply lines and operations

Even though [factions](factions/Factions.md) keep their existance top secret, the idea of a striker role natually emerged among many (if not all) factions. Such comparitmentalisation influenced strikers' development as a whole into taking peculiar and unique design choices resulting in strikers from different factions being completely different

Despite how useful they have been proven, the cost of designing such a secret product line limited factions to only a single striker chassie and one, or rarely two configurations

---

[^1]: Umbrella term because every Faction has a different nomenclature
