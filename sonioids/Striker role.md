# Striker role

Striker is the umbrella term[^1] to describe combat Sonioids. They are generally fast and stealthy with the software and hardware needed to damage what they deem a threat.

Built at first with refurbished [explorer](sonioids/Explorer%20role.md) chassis to perform industrial espionage, they later became an internal product line specialized in espionage, combat missions, and strategic disruptions of opponents' supply lines and operations.

Even though [factions](factions/Factions.md) keep their existence top secret, the idea of a striker role naturally emerged among many (if not all) factions. Such compartmentalization influenced strikers' development as a whole into taking peculiar and unique design choices, resulting in strikers from different factions being completely unique.

Despite how useful they have been proven, the cost of designing such a secret product line encouraged factions to design only a single striker chassis and one, or rarely two configurations

[^1]: Umbrella term because every Faction has a different nomenclature
