# Research Class

---

Large, Komodo dragon-esc robots built to carry large loads

They have claws on their primarily appendages intended for digging, with two smaller arms located near the top of their chest intended for analysis of specimens.

They fill these roles in their packs:

- Digging nesting burrows
- carrying specimens and necessary resources
- analyzing specimens

Research class sonioids are sedentary after digging a nest, given their large size and the larger processors, they need to preserve energy for analyzing specimens the explorer class sonioids bring to the nest, so they don't move more than necessary.

While sonioids are adverse to combat and confrontation, research class sonioids will use their large digging claws as weapons if backed into a corner. Their pneumatic structure in their front limbs (usually used to break apart tough rock) are effective at dealing with threats.

Originally, research class sonioids were intended to be used as "checkpoints" in the open oceans on Earth that explorer class sonioids would return to in order to recharge and drop off anything found. After interstellar space travel was invented, they were repurposed for space exploration and research, given sonioids affinity for high pressure environments with little to not light.

Research class sonioids are also equipped with an internal fabricator that takes organic material and converts it to usable cabling that can withstand the pressures of space and deep oceans. Research class sonioids attach these cables to explorer class sonioids before they dig a nest in order to avoid lagging behind and potentially getting destroyed by hostile sonioid factions (companies).

Research class sonioids are largely take design inspiration from spiders and large lizards like crocodiles and Komodo dragons. Although they also take features from aquatic and amphibious animals as well.
