# Sonioids

Sonioids use geothermal energy to recharge

Sonioids can also absorb and store methane and hydrogen from the atmosphere and convert them into usable fuel if there is no way for them to generate energy from the planet's heat.

Sonioids use primarily ultrasound/sonar in order to "see", but in low/no atmosphere environments, they can also utilize lidar (this was a later addition after the first generation of space-faring sonioids were deployed)

Sonioids can also utilize their lidar to communicate over long distances with other sonioids or man-made communication stations, although contact with humanity is infrequent, and sending data and specimens back is automated in all but the oldest deployments.

They come in two primarily roles (legacy classes):

- [Research role](sonioids/Research%20role.md)
- [Explorer role](sonioids/Explorer%20role.md)
- [Striker role](sonioids/Striker%20role.md)

They are distinguished by their class (operating environment):

- Aquatic
- Underground
- Land
