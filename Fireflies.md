# "Fireflies"

*Fireflies* are the codename for a species of insect on one of the planets the Sonioids are deployed on, specifically sonioids #o77x78 and #7ygp5h, manufactured by [Ambient Frontier](Ambient%20Frontier.md). 

---
\[Begin Report\]

#Report-j5v7jp579

*Fireflies* are a large insect with an extreme sensitivity to light and the ability to produce a highly corrosive substance known to burn through aluminum and steel. Because of this, #o77x78 and #7ygp5h created ceramic plating using #7ygp5h's built-in fabricator and materials gathered from the planet. This ceramic plating is resistant to the corrosive substance, but not effective against the insects' large numbers and extreme aggression. #o77x78 has observed the insects and noted that they cannot perceive ultrasound/sonar, but are incredibly sensitive to the lidar arrays equipped on both units.

#o77x78 and #7ygp5h were originally in a squad of 6 total sonioids, 2 researchers and 4 explorers, all 6 of them were Dwarf Class, as they were tasked with gathering information on the materials common to the planet for mining viability. Unfortunately, all but units #o77x78 and #7ygp5h were destroyed by the insects.

As of now, Ambient Frontier has no intention of retrieving the two remaining units, and has continued to task them with communicating back information regarding the mineral composition of the planet.
